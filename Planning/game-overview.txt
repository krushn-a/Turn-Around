GAME TITLE: Turn Around ?
THEME: Fear
ENGINE: Hytopia (Voxel Sandbox)
MODE: Single Player
DURATION: 5–10 minutes

------------------------------------------------------------
1. CONCEPT OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------
The player wakes up in a long, dimly lit tunnel.
They are told only one rule: “Don’t turn around.”
As they walk deeper, whispers, footsteps, and strange lights try to tempt them.
If they turn around even once — the game ends.
If they reach the exit without turning, they survive.

Core Emotion: Suspense, restraint, and psychological tension.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Player spawns at tunnel entrance.
2. On-screen message: "Don’t turn around."
3. Player walks forward through checkpoints.
4. Each checkpoint triggers new sounds or visuals behind them.
5. If player turns back → immediate bad ending.
6. If player reaches last checkpoint → good ending.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. MAIN GAMEPLAY MECHANICS
------------------------------------------------------------
- **Movement:** Forward-only exploration. No interactions or combat.
- **Rotation Detection:** Track player facing direction. Turning beyond ±90° from forward triggers fail state.
- **Checkpoint Events:** Each checkpoint activates unique environmental or audio effects.
- **End Conditions:**
  - SUCCESS: Player reaches final checkpoint.
  - FAILURE: Player turns around.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. LEVEL DESIGN
------------------------------------------------------------
TUNNEL STRUCTURE:
- Length: 250–300 blocks long
- Width: 3 blocks
- Height: 4 blocks
- Material: Dark stone or obsidian blocks
- Shape: Slightly curved or uneven for realism
- Lighting: Starts faintly bright, dims gradually at each checkpoint
- Fog: Medium density to obscure far distance

DECORATION:
- Flickering lights and sparse lanterns
- Faint wall writings (e.g., “Don’t.” / “He’s behind you.”)
- Cracked floor or debris particles
- Dripping water sound or dust motes

------------------------------------------------------------
5. SOUND DESIGN PLAN
------------------------------------------------------------
Atmosphere depends on layered sound cues.

GLOBAL:
- Low ambient hum or wind tone loop.

CHECKPOINT-BASED AUDIO EVENTS:
| Checkpoint | Event | Description |
|-------------|--------|-------------|
| CP1 | Whisper: “Hey…” | Soft introduction, far behind. |
| CP2 | Whisper: “Wait…” + small block fall sound | First temptation. |
| CP3 | Footsteps start matching player’s pace. | Follows rhythmically. |
| CP4 | Crying sound + flickering lights. | Emotional lure. |
| CP5 | Heavy breathing directly behind. | Intense tension. |
| CP6 | “Please… look at me.” whisper + wall shake. | Breaking point. |
| CP7 | Loud metal scrape or knock. | Final scare before exit. |
| CP8 | Exit glow visible, calm ambient replaces hum. | Relief phase. |

POSITIONAL AUDIO:
- All key sounds originate 2–3 blocks behind the player’s starting orientation.
- Volume increases slightly with each checkpoint to simulate proximity.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. VISUAL TEMPTATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
- Random light flickers from behind.
- Shadow flicker that moves when player isn’t looking.
- Particle spark or dust fall sound.
- Reflection of something in puddles or glossy tiles.
- Small object falls, luring curiosity.
- Red scratch text: “TURN” or “HELP” near late checkpoints.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. CHECKPOINT FLOW
------------------------------------------------------------
CP0 — Start: Fade in, display “Don’t turn around.”
CP1 — Whisper starts, ambient hum fades in.
CP2 — Light flickers, faint noise behind.
CP3 — Footsteps sync with player.
CP4 — Crying or call for help sound.
CP5 — Heavy breathing close to ear.
CP6 — Wall vibration, tunnel rumble, whisper: “Look at me.”
CP7 — Loud metal scrape or crash, short silence.
CP8 — Light appears ahead, calming tone starts.
Exit — Fade to white or black depending on outcome.

------------------------------------------------------------
8. ENDINGS
------------------------------------------------------------
BAD END (Player Turned Around):
- Screen instantly fades to black.
- One-frame distorted face flash.
- Whisper: “I told you not to.”
- Credits fade in silently.

GOOD END (Player Reached Exit):
- Tunnel brightens.
- Calm ambient replaces hum.
- Text: “You made it.”
- Fade out → end screen / credits.

------------------------------------------------------------
9. SCRIPTING BLUEPRINT (PSEUDOCODE STYLE)
------------------------------------------------------------
Variables:
- startFacingDirection (float)
- rotationThreshold = 90 degrees
- currentCheckpoint = 0
- checkpoints[] = { positions of CP1–CP8 }

Init():
  - Save startFacingDirection = player.rotation.y
  - Display text: “Don’t turn around.”
  - Start ambient hum.

OnUpdate():
  - If abs(player.rotation.y - startFacingDirection) > rotationThreshold:
        TriggerBadEnd()

  - For each checkpoint in checkpoints:
        If player passes checkpoint and checkpoint not triggered:
            TriggerCheckpointEvent(checkpoint)
            currentCheckpoint++

  - If player reaches final checkpoint:
        TriggerGoodEnd()

TriggerCheckpointEvent(checkpoint):
  - Play assigned sound (positional behind player)
  - Trigger light flicker / particle / text if defined
  - Optional: Camera shake or visual distortion

TriggerBadEnd():
  - Stop ambient sounds
  - Play distortion + whisper
  - Fade to black
  - Display “I told you not to.”

TriggerGoodEnd():
  - Stop hum
  - Fade to white
  - Display “You made it.”
